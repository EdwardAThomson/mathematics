## 2026-08-07

The project's biggest day: both rigor forks ran end to end. On the Rayo/truth fork, R0 delivered a well-definedness verdict for Rayo's own construction, the load-bearing Morse-Kelley truth-class claim was verified against the literature (the "surprising weakness of KM" paper does not undercut it), and RAYO-GROWTH-RATE.md analyzed how fast Rayo grows: it dominates every V-definable total function, passes the fast-growing hierarchy at every fixed ordinal notation, and by Tarski is itself not definable-total. In rayo-lean, Stage 1 Gödel-coded formulas into HF with a proved round-trip; the reuse-compression check for k=6 was proved; and a successor-chain naming of 6 was mechanized at 388 symbols vs 11,128 for the enumerate-predecessors K6 (28.7x cheaper), confirming the O(k) prediction. On the Boolos/provability fork, B0's well-definedness paper proof, B1's TNames formalization, and B3 all landed: after one session got stuck on a substitution lemma, the missing Foundation fact (Rew.q_subst, not simp-tagged) unblocked provablyTotal_names; BoolosBig_PA was then built as a real function with an honest symbol-size measure (Foundation's complexity measure ignores terms entirely, a landmine avoided); the last fSize-under-substitution bridging lemma closed; and the full domination theorem BoolosBig_PA_dominates went through sorry-free via the n-squared pre-inflation trick. A consolidating large-numbers/README.md tied the 17 notes into one narrative, and a paper-verification pass on B3 caught a real gap (the additive-constant form was unjustified; squaring fixes it) before the mechanization targeted it.

**Decisions & notes:** The throughline crystallized: Boolos and Rayo are the same naming machine with different fuel (provability-in-PA vs truth-in-V), and the growth gap traces entirely to the strength of what "names" means. A Classical.choice audit corrected an earlier "never uses choice" claim: K2-K6 genuinely use it via Classical.em; only K0/K1 are choice-free. The symbol-count gap against an automated counter was traced to an unavoidable ambiguity in the not-forall-not encoding of exists and judged in favor of the documented counts. Work split across sessions via a version-controlled laptop handoff doc with acceptance criteria (sorry-free, #print axioms).
